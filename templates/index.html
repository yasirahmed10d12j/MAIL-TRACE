MailTrace

Know if your email was opened, when, where in the world, and on which device — the same tracking-pixel technology used by Mailtrack, Yesware and sales-intelligence tools. Includes automated bot & email-proxy detection (Gmail/Outlook proxy images through their own servers — MailTrace flags those opens so your data stays honest).

How it works

  1. Register an email in the dashboard → you get a unique tracking pixel /pixel/<id>.gif
  2. Embed the pixel in your email's HTML (or send directly via SMTP from the app)
  3. When the email is opened, the pixel fetch is logged: timestamp, IP, location, device
  4. Watch opens appear live on the dashboard map & timeline
Built as a cybersecurity portfolio project by Ahmed.
⚠️ Ethical use only: track only emails you send, and respect privacy laws (GDPR etc.). Note: Gmail and Outlook proxy images through their own infrastructure — MailTrace detects and labels these as proxy opens.
